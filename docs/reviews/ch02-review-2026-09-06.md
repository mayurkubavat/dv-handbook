# Verdict

**Needs another draft pass.** The chapter is strong where it rests on OpenTitan
and the two DVCon papers: every quotation I checked matches the research note
word for word and carries a same-line citation, the AI results in the Future
Directions box are reported with the right caveats, and the report tool does
what the prose says it does (3 passing, 4 failing, 5 with no evidence; the
expect-fail inversion is real and correct). The problems are of three kinds: a
handful of claims the note marks moderate-confidence or does not support at all
are printed as fact; the worked example's report labels items "passing" whose
own closure criteria are not met, and the prose does not say so; and the
included files break two house rules (80 columns, American spelling) while the
chapter's section order breaks the template.

The three most serious findings, in order:

1. **[T-1, "The report" section, line 182 and 184] Items 001 to 003 are
   reported "passing" although their closure criteria are not met, and the
   prose repeats the label as if it were closure.** The plan's own closure
   for FIFO-002 and FIFO-003 is "all DEPTH occupancy bins hit", and FIFO-001
   wants a reset observed with the FIFO non-empty. The directed test reaches
   occupancy 0 to 8 and resets once, from empty. The tool consults only the
   test pass/fail column and never reads `coverage` or `closure`. The chapter
   then says "Three items pass" and "It said which requirements the passing
   test actually covered", which is exactly the confusion the chapter warns
   against on line 33. Fix: rename the state to "tests passing" (or add a
   sentence stating that the tool measures test evidence only and that
   closure additionally needs the coverage column, which Part II's coverage
   chapter will add), and correct line 184 to say the report shows which
   items have a passing test, not which requirements are covered.

2. **[U-11, line 139] "OpenTitan chose 90 and 100 percent, one hundred seeds
   and a one-week soak before any block existed"** is a historical claim with
   no source. The note only establishes that the numbers are in today's
   development-stages page and checklist; nothing says when they were set. The
   sentence carries the section's whole argument ("written at V0"). Fix: "The
   numbers are written into the project's stage definitions and applied to
   every block" [@opentitan-dev-stages; @opentitan-checklist], dropping the
   claim about when.

3. **[T-4, line 137] "the paper that introduced the term"** (suspect link) is
   wrong. The note says Kreisinger and Chatterjee use "the tool's *suspect
   link* mechanism", meaning Jama Connect's built-in feature; the paper adopts
   the term, it does not coin it. Fix: "is stated by the traceability paper
   cited above as a necessary condition for closure".
