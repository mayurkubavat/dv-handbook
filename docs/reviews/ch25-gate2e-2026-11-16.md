## Non-blocking findings

**N1. Line 39: the bin that fills on the repaired FIFO is not printed.**
"on the repaired FIFO it fills" is the right explanation and the
arithmetic agrees (one bin of 29 between 86.21 and 89.66), but no output
the chapter or the book prints shows `full_16` hit on `fifo_fixed.sv`:
`opening/run.out` prints only the two percentages, and the fixer's
check (`research/ch25-draft-log.md` section 9, "`full_16=136`") was a
scratch run, not committed. A reader who wants to verify the sentence
cannot. Fix, either: have `opening` print the push-biased run's
`occupancy:` line beside its percentage and splice it; or soften to
"the one bin that differs is the one for sixteen entries, which the
monitor counts only while `full` is low ...". Not blocking: the claim is
true, consistent with the testbench code and with Chapter 8's own
account.

**N2. Line 446: "reruns four rows, shown further on".** The default run
now also runs `a_integrity` alone on the fourteen misses (`run.out`
40-43), which line 464 introduces. The sentence is still true but
undersells what is shown further on. Fix: "reruns four rows and gives
the fourteen misses more time, both shown further on".

**N3. Line 533: "the five survivors at the ports are the five equivalent
mutants".** The clause follows "36 of 39 at default values and 39 of 39
with randomized ones" and applies only to the randomized run; at default
values the port survivors are eight (`fifth-tb/run.out:26`: `m05 m09 m11
m13 m32 m36 m43 m44`), and line 535 then speaks of seven survivors
(those of the shipped checker plus `Sync_A`). Fix: "with randomized
values, the five survivors at the ports are the five equivalent
mutants".
