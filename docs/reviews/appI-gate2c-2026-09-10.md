## B-1. `appendices/appI-agent-tool-layer.qmd:248` — a citation is swallowed by the cross-reference before it, and the printed page carries an uncited claim about a named tool

Source:

The citation is gone. `@key [suffix]` is pandoc's in-text citation form, so
`@sec-appI-ast [@treesitter-sv]` parses as the citation `sec-appI-ast` with
`[@treesitter-sv]` as its suffix; Quarto resolves the crossref and the suffix
is discarded. Nothing warns. AGENTS.md: "Every number, benchmark or claim
about a tool has a source: `[@key]` in `refs.bib`." The key is in the `.qmd`
and does not reach the reader. `treesitter-sv` still appears in the printed
bibliography only because `:352` cites it again in Further reading, which is
what makes this invisible to a key-resolution check — all 32 keys resolve, and
one of them prints nowhere on the page that needs it.

The construction is not unique to this line. `chapters/ch02-verification-planning.qmd:142`
has `@sec-ch02-coverage [@opentitan-checklist]`, and the PDF (page 30) prints
"with the waiver rules of Section 2.5." with no citation. That one is Chapter
2's finding, not this appendix's, but it shows the failure is systematic.

**Proposed fix.** Separate the two so neither is a suffix of the other:

> …and tree-sitter has a SystemVerilog grammar [@treesitter-sv] for the
> error-tolerant, editor-time half of @sec-appI-ast.

and add to the release checklist, since nothing else will catch it:
`grep -rnE '@(sec|tbl|fig)-[A-Za-z0-9-]+ \[@' chapters/ appendices/` must be
empty.
