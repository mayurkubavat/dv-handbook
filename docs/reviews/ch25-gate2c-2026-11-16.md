## Blocking findings

**C-B1. Lines 34 and 39: the covergroup does not report "the same
percentages ... on any FIFO, correct or broken", and the chapter's next
paragraph says so.**

Line 34: "Its covergroup reports the same percentages on the repaired FIFO
as it would on any FIFO, correct or broken". Line 39: "These are the
percentages on the repaired FIFO, which differ from the ones
@sec-ch08-example printed for the original."

The covergroup samples the design's own flags (`ch08-functional-coverage/
fifo-coverage/tb_fifo_cov.sv:27-30, 52`: `full_cp`, `empty_cp` and their
crosses with the operation), so its percentages depend on the design.
Chapter 8's run on the original FIFO (`fifo-coverage/run.out` lines 2 and
15) prints 75.86 for the even mix and 86.21 for the push-biased mix; this
chapter's run on the repaired FIFO (`opening/run.out` 7-8, spliced at 36)
prints 75.86 and 89.66. So line 34 is false, line 39 contradicts it five
lines later, and line 39 is itself half right: the even-mix figure is
identical, only the push-biased one differs. A reader who compares the
two chapters sees a claim the book's own outputs refute, in the paragraph
that sets up the chapter's thesis. (The sentence predates round 1; it is
unchanged since `081b5c7`.)

The data make the chapter's point better than the sentence does. Fix,
for example: line 34, "Its covergroup reports percentages and never a
failure, whether the FIFO is correct or broken:"; line 39, "The even mix
gives the same 75.86 that @sec-ch08-example printed for the original; the
push-biased mix gives 89.66 against the original's 86.21, because the
covergroup samples the design's flags and the repaired FIFO reaches full
where the original raised it a word early. The coverage moved when the
design changed, and still said nothing about which design was right."
(Check the last clause's mechanism against Chapter 8's own explanation
of the push-while-full bin before printing it.)

## Non-blocking findings

**N1. Line 551 (summary): "the random test with the same coverage kills
35".** Residue of gate 2b B2 and gate 2a N1, whose fix named this line.
The clause before it lists line, branch and expression points, so the
reading is defensible, but the toggles differ (329 against 326 of 330).
Fix: "the random test, with the same line, branch and expression
coverage, kills 35".

**N2. Line 157 and the axes figure: "the testbench as it ships".** The
random test is now this chapter's copy (line 23; `source-sim/run.out` 4
says "this chapter's copy"). Line 157 still says "the testbench as it
ships", and the axes figure's footnote (`gen_ch25_figures.py:301`, in
`ch25-axes.svg`) says "each testbench was run as it ships". Fix: "this
chapter's copy of the random test, at `+seed=1` and 400 cycles"; "each
testbench was run as the chapter configures it".

**N3. Line 23: "The FIFO of @sec-ch01-example never fills".** Its `full`
flag does rise, at fifteen entries; that is the bug. What it never does
is hold sixteen entries, which is what the copy's header says
(`common/tb_fifo_random.sv:7`). Fix: "never holds sixteen entries".

**N4. Lines 519-521 and 530: the Step 3 splice runs into Step 5's
output.** `fifth-tb/run.out` 7-24 ends with the heading "4. kills at the
ports: the fifth testbench with Sync_A tied to / the checker's own
count", which Step 5 splices again (23-29). Fix: splice 7-22 at 521.

**N5. `netlist-formal/run.out` 32-33 (spliced at 461): "the mutations
a_integrity kills differ at depth 15 or less (all its kills: 18 or
less)".** "Its" reads as `a_integrity`'s, but the 18 is the property
set's (`nformal.py:117-118, 129-132`: `max(hit)` over every row with any
property; from `measure.out` the deepest `a_integrity`-killed mutation
differs at 15, the deepest set-killed one at 18, rows 2039 and 2083,
killed by flag properties). Line 464 reads it correctly; the printed line
does not. Fix in `nformal.py`: "(the property set's kills: 18 or less)".

**N6. Line 464: "which is what a bound of three seconds of bounded search
for each property looks like on this design".** The reach of three
seconds depends on the property: on row 2083 `EmptyExact_A` is refuted
at depth 33 within its three seconds (`netlist-formal/measure.out:27`).
The 15/16 split is `a_integrity`'s reach. Fix: "what three seconds of
bounded search on `a_integrity` reaches on this design".

**N7. Lines 327, 459 and 557: "every one of them is a bound and not a
gap" is inferred, not shown.** The evidence is the depth split and the
location of the fourteen (memory write and read paths, `a_integrity`'s
domain); no run shows a property failing on any of them. The inference
is strong, but the chapter states it as a finding. Either soften once
("all fourteen look like bounds, not gaps: ...") or demonstrate it on one
or two, for example `a_integrity` with a longer limit on 8096 (miter
depth 16) or 8105.

**N8. Line 533: the netlist figure is not split into port and white-box
kills.** The sentence opens "with the white-box kill apart", and does so
for the source mutants, but its 899 of 1,000 comes from the fifth
testbench with `Sync_A` live (`fifth-tb/run.out` 22); the port-only run
(`fifo_port_bind.sv`, run.out 23-29) was made on the source mutants only.
The number of netlist mutations that only `Sync_A` kills is probably
small (the formal sample's six equivalents are killed by nothing,
`netlist-formal/run.out` 15), but the chapter's own rule asks for it.
Fix: run the port bind on the sample and print both, or add "including
any kills by `Sync_A`" after the 899.

**N9. Line 488: "The commercial tool those papers use, Certitude".**
"Those papers" takes in Huang et al., read at abstract level
(`huang2015mutation` note; research note lines 491-493), with no record
that they use Certitude. Fix: "The commercial tool Lin and colleagues
use, Certitude, ...".

**N10. Line 309: "its first counterexample is eighteen cycles deep".**
True of the first property tried that fails (`FullBackward_A@18`,
`property-set/run.out` 49), but `FullForward_A` fails at 17
(`property-set/measure.out:62`) and the miter differs at 17 (line 229).
A reader who checks the measured row sees a shallower counterexample.
Fix: "the first property tried that fails it, `FullBackward_A`, needs
eighteen cycles".

**N11. Line 245: "A testbench that does not pulses the reset between two
edges".** Garbled. Fix: "A testbench that does not, and instead pulses
the reset between two edges and looks at the FIFO before the next one,
tells them apart:".

**N12. Line 440: "Its output may be a good set of mutations to run".**
The round-1 sentence on the hypergeometric draw was inserted between this
sentence and its antecedent (`mutate -list N`), so "its" now reads as the
draw without replacement. Fix: "The output of `mutate -list N` may be
...".

**N13. Line 78 (alt text): "towards reveal".** American usage is
"toward".
