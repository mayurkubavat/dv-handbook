# Verdict

**Needs another draft pass.** The chapter is well argued and the numbers that
matter most (14/24/32 percent first-silicon success, 47/49 percent, 6.2/2.7
percent CAGR, $475M, $733M, the IBS design-cost figures, the Zenbleed
timeline, the FDIV mechanism) match the research note. The problems are in
three places: a handful of claims that the note marks UNVERIFIED or does not
cover at all are printed as fact without a citation; the chapter is written in
British spelling against an American-spelling house rule; and the worked
example's verification status is incomplete because `status.json` records only
one of the three examples. Three code-versus-prose mismatches are small but
real.

The three most serious findings, in order:

1. **[U-7, line 101] The Cougar Point root cause is printed without a
   source.** The note marks the "transistor in a clock circuit biased at too
   high a voltage" detail UNVERIFIED (AnandTech and the Intel newsroom page
   were not retrievable) and says to attribute it to "Intel's January 2011
   briefing as reported in the trade press" if used. The chapter attributes
   it to "trade-press accounts" but gives no `[@key]`, and no such entry
   exists in `refs.bib`. House rule: every claim about a named product has a
   source.
2. **[E-1, examples/status.json] Only `sv-directed` has been checked.**
   `status.json` lists one example (pass 1, fail 0). The two examples whose
   output the chapter prints verbatim, `sv-random` and `cocotb`, are absent.
   Their `run.log` files exist and match the printed output, but the
   pipeline's own record does not cover them, so the chapter cannot reach
   `examples-verified` on this evidence. Re-run the checker over the whole
   chapter directory.
3. **[H-1, whole chapter] British spelling throughout, against the house
   rule "American, consistently".** Seventeen instances: behaviour (x9),
   flavours, flavoured, serialising, publicised, labelled, organised,
   honours, Recognise. Mechanical to fix, but it is a stated rule.

The remaining findings are listed by category below. Line numbers refer to
`chapters/ch01-what-is-dv.qmd` as read on 2026-09-06.

# 7. Verdict

**Needs another draft pass.** Nothing here requires re-research; every fix is
either a citation to add, a sentence to cut or hedge, a spelling pass, or a
re-run of the example checker. The three most serious findings are U-7
(Cougar Point root cause printed without a source, against an UNVERIFIED mark
in the note), E-1 (the example verification record covers one of three
examples), and H-1 (British spelling against a stated house rule). After
those, the next tier is U-8 (a citation pointing at a document that does not
contain the fact), U-15 (an inference labelled as a measurement in the Future
Directions callout), A-1/A-2/T-1 (Ormandy's words unquoted, and his method
misdescribed in the opening), and T-4 (the "15 of 17" coverage line is
unexplained and reveals a hole in the coverage model the chapter should either
fix or teach).
