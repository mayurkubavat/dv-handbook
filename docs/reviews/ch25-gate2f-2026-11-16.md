## Non-blocking findings

### N1. The new `a_integrity` reruns have a margin of about 3.5 times, not more

`examples/ch25-mutation-testing/netlist-formal/nformal.py:144-160`
(`integrity()`: bounded search 30 s, then rIC3 120 s), pinned at
`nformal.py:251-257`. On this 4-core container, with the run's own four
workers, the slowest bounded-search refutation is 7648 at depth 18 in
8.52 s and 8.35 s (limit 30 s), and the slower rIC3 refutation is 8394
in 33.60 s and 32.75 s (limit 120 s): about 3.5 times in both. With four
busy loops on the four cores (roughly half speed) the same jobs took
17.47 s and 52.58 s and the output was still identical (E3). So a
machine about three to four times slower than this one would send 7648
to rIC3, and the pin "bounded search refutes 12" would fail. That is
an ordinary margin for the book's formal examples and nothing in the
three runs came near it, so it is not blocking. The other direction is
safe: a 90 s bounded search on 7759 and 8394 still finds nothing (E3),
so a faster machine will not move those two to bounded search. If a
wider margin is wanted, raising the bounded limit to 60 s costs about
30 s of wall time (only the two deep jobs use the whole limit, and they
run in parallel) and doubles the margin of the twelve.

### N2. The fourteen misses are just past three seconds, so the measured count depends on the machine

The new run shows how close the measured bound is: the twelve misses at
depth 16-18 are refuted by `a_integrity` in 3.10 to 8.52 s, eight of
them in under 4.5 s (E2), against the measured run's 3 s for each
property (`netlist-formal/measure.out:3`, spliced via `run.out:45`). A
`MEASURE=1` run on a machine about 1.5 times faster would probably
miss fewer than fourteen, and on a slower one more. The default run is
not affected, because it pins `measure.out` and recomputes from it; and
the chapter already frames the result as a bound of the search (qmd:464
"what three seconds of bounded search on `a_integrity` reaches on this
design", qmd:472 "took this machine"). Optional: say at qmd:464 that the
count of misses is a property of the search's time on this machine, not
of the design, which the new run makes plain.
