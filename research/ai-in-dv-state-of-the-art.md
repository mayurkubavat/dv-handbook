## 0. Framing and how to read this note

- **Three generations coexist in 2026.** (a) Classical ML on verification data (test ranking, coverage prediction, failure clustering) that shipped in 2022–2024 products; (b) LLM copilots for code, assertions, and debug summaries (2023–2025); (c) agentic flows that run simulators and formal tools in a loop and iterate on tests (2025–2026). Vendors now market all three under "autonomy levels".
- **Marketing vs evidence.** Vendor figures ("10X", "40X", "50X") are unaudited, baseline-free, and usually from one named customer. Peer-reviewed numbers come from small open designs, are measured by pass rate or coverage, and rarely include bug-finding power. Both are labeled below: **[vendor]** or **[peer-reviewed]** / **[preprint]**.
- **Bottom line for the textbook.** The DV engineer's core skills (spec reading, test planning, coverage judgement, root-cause reasoning, sign-off) are the ones the tools try to automate and the ones the tools still depend on a human to check. The 2024 Wilson Research Group study reported first-silicon success at a two-decade low (about 14 percent of IC/ASIC projects), which is the demand driver every vendor cites [Siemens / Wilson Research Group 2024 IC/ASIC report](https://resources.sw.siemens.com/en-US/white-paper-2024-wilson-research-group-ic-asic-functional-verification-trend-report/).

## 1. Commercial tools

## 2. Academic and open research

### 2.1 RTL generation benchmarks (context for DV)

- **VerilogEval v2** (NVIDIA/Cornell, TODAES 2025) [peer-reviewed]: 156 to 209 problems, spec-to-RTL; GPT-4o 63 percent, Llama 3.1 405B 58 percent, RTL-Coder 6.7B 34 percent; prompt format changes results materially [arXiv 2408.11053](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11053). **RTLLM v2** has 50 designs.
- **Saturation and contamination.** Frontier models now score up to about 90 percent on VerilogEval and 96 percent on RTLLM [TuRTLe, 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.01986); **VeriContaminated** (2025-03) finds evidence of training-data contamination on both benchmarks with CCD and Min-K% probes [arXiv 2503.13572](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13572).
- **Harder successors.** **CVDP** (NVIDIA, 2025-06): 783 engineer-authored problems in 13 categories including verification and debug, agentic and non-agentic forms; best pass@1 34 percent (Claude 3.7 Sonnet), agentic tasks harder [arXiv 2506.14074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14074), [GitHub NVlabs/cvdp_benchmark](https://github.com/NVlabs/cvdp_benchmark). **ChipBench** (2026-01): 44 hierarchical modules, 89 debug scenarios, 132 reference-model tasks; Claude 4.5 Opus 30.74 percent on generation and 13.33 percent on Python reference models [arXiv 2601.21448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21448).
- **Textbook relevance:** teach students that "95 percent on VerilogEval" measures toy modules; CVDP/ChipBench numbers are the honest picture.

### 2.2 Assertion generation and LLM + formal loops

- **AssertLLM** (ASP-DAC 2025) [peer-reviewed]: three cooperating LLMs extract structure from spec text and waveform diagrams and emit SVA [ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3658617.3697756). **AssertLLM2** (2026-05) [preprint]: 83 real designs, evaluated by formal provability, coverage and mutation-based bug detection; first to include buggy RTL as input [arXiv 2605.27472](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27472).
- **FVEval** (NVIDIA/UC Berkeley, 2024-10): NL2SVA-Human (79 expert specs), NL2SVA-Machine, Design2SVA; scored with industrial formal tools [arXiv 2410.23299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23299), [GitHub NVlabs/FVEval](https://github.com/NVlabs/FVEval).
- **VERT dataset** (Intel and UT Dallas authors, 2025-03, TODAES): synthetic code/SVA pairs; fine-tuned Deepseek-Coder 6.7B and Llama 3.1 8B beat GPT-4o by up to 24.14 percent on OpenTitan, CVA6, OpenPiton, Pulpissimo [arXiv 2503.08923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08923).
- **Solver-in-the-loop agents.** **ProofLoop** (2026-04) is a ReAct agent that queries JasperGold for structure, then refines SVA against proof feedback for up to three rounds: 93.7 percent syntax and 82.0 percent functional correctness on FVEval Design2SVA [arXiv 2604.23100](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23100). **Quality-aware closure** (2026-06) adds vacuity rejection, mutation-guided refinement and solver selection, arguing that closure, not generation, is the missing step [arXiv 2606.21451](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21451). Related: CoverAssert (coverage-driven iteration) [arXiv 2604.06607](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.06607), STELLAR (structure-guided retrieval) [arXiv 2601.19903](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.19903), AssertionForge [arXiv 2503.19174](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.19174).
- **Debugging formal failures.** **FVDebug** (NVIDIA, 2025-09): causal-graph synthesis over counterexample traces, "for-and-against" LLM prompting, agentic exploration, fix generation; validated on open benchmarks and two production counterexamples [arXiv 2510.15906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15906).
- **Textbook relevance:** the pattern "LLM proposes, formal tool disposes, mutation checks strength" is the reusable methodology; vacuous and wrong-reason assertions are the failure mode to teach.

### 2.3 UVM testbench, test plan, and stimulus generation

- **UVM2 / "UVM Machine"** (ICCAD 2025) [peer-reviewed]: analysis, generation and optimisation agents; average 87.44 percent code and 89.58 percent functional coverage on designs up to 1.6K lines, about 21–24 points above prior LLM baselines [arXiv 2504.19959](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19959). **UVMarvel** (2026-05) extends to subsystem level with a bus-protocol library and IR [arXiv 2605.04704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04704).
- **HAVEN** (2026-04) [preprint]: avoids direct HDL generation; LLM plans, Jinja2 protocol templates emit UVM, a sequence DSL drives coverage iteration; 19 open IPs (Direct, Wishbone, AXI4-Lite), 100 percent compile, 90.6 percent code and 87.9 percent functional coverage [arXiv 2604.27643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27643).
- **Benchmarks for testbench quality.** **VerifLLMBench** (Univ. of Minnesota, DVCon US 2025): UVM testbench generation from NL with GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Llama 3.2, prompt-repair loop and coverage analysis [DVCon 2025 paper PDF](https://people.ece.umn.edu/users/jsartori/papers/dvcon25.pdf). **FIXME** (2025-07): 180 tasks, six DV sub-domains, three difficulty levels, silicon-proven designs; open at ChatDesignVerification/FIXME [arXiv 2507.04276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04276).
- **Test-plan generation is weak.** With GRPO plus state mutations (2026-01), DeepSeek-R1 and Claude 4 Sonnet pass golden RTL only 15.7–21.7 percent of the time; a trained 7B model reaches 33.3 percent golden pass and a 13.9 percent mutation-detection rate [arXiv 2601.07593](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07593). Security test-plan generation from specs: **ThreatLens** (VTS 2025) on NEORV32 [arXiv 2505.06821](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.06821).
- **Training for coverage.** **LLM4Cov** (NVIDIA/UCSD, ICML 2026): execution-validated data curation and worst-state sampling let a 4B model hit 69.2 percent pass and 90.4 percent coverage on CVDP-ECov [arXiv 2602.16953](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16953).
- **Textbook relevance:** high code coverage on small IPs is now routine for LLM testbenches; low mutation-detection and golden-pass rates show that checkers and test plans, not stimulus, remain the hard part.

### 2.4 LLM-based debug and root cause

- **Root cause of sim/synthesis failures** (Pearce et al., 2025-07): 34 buggy scenarios; o3-mini correct 100 percent at pass@5, most models above 80 percent, above 90 percent with RAG; small corpus [arXiv 2507.06512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06512). **ChipBench** debug track and **FVDebug** above. Industrial: ChipAgents Waveform Agents, Bronco AI, Cadence Verisium Debug (section 1).
- **Textbook relevance:** debug is where LLMs help most today because the output is a hypothesis a human checks, not code that must be right.

### 2.5 Domain models and datasets

- Fine-tune lineage: VeriGen (TODAES 2024), RTLCoder, CodeV, MG-Verilog (multi-grained descriptions) [arXiv 2407.01910](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.01910), DeepRTL (understanding plus generation, ICLR 2025) [arXiv 2502.15832](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.15832), CraftRTL, OpenRTLSet (fully open, 2026-06) [arXiv 2606.10285](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10285), DeepCircuitX (repository-level) [arXiv 2502.18297](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.18297). Verification-specific: VERT (SVA), CVDP, FIXME, AssertLLM2, FVEval.
- "Small models plus agents" (2025-12) shows agentic decomposition lets small open models approach frontier performance on CVDP [arXiv 2512.05073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05073), but "Exploring the Agentic Frontier" (2026-03) warns that naive agent wrappers can *degrade* frontier models and that open models suffer higher crash rates and weaker tool-output interpretation [arXiv 2603.19347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19347).

## 3. Agentic DV flows

- **Open closed-loop agents.** **AgentDV** (2026-08) [preprint]: LLM writes cocotb tests, runs Verilator, reads coverage, gets annotated uncovered regions, iterates; on OpenTitan peripheral and security IPs Claude Sonnet 4.6 achieved 80.9 percent average pass with 74.5 percent line and 88.4 percent branch coverage; single-shot prompting failed to produce any coverage-producing environment [arXiv 2608.27148](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27148). **Architect in the Loop** (2025-10): agents decompose a LEGv8 and an RV32 core, generate HDL and cocotb tests, human intervenes at debug and synthesis; about one million tokens per processor [arXiv 2512.00016](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00016).
- **MCP servers for EDA.** **MCP4EDA** exposes Yosys, Icarus, VCD parsing, LibreLane and KLayout to Claude Desktop or Cursor [GitHub NellyW8/MCP4EDA](https://github.com/NellyW8/MCP4EDA); **AutoEDA** (2025-08) pairs MCP microservices with locally fine-tuned models for privacy [arXiv 2508.01012](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.01012); commercial: AMIQ DVT MCP Server, Siemens Questa One toolkit usable from Claude Code/Cursor (section 1).
- **Vendor agent stacks.** Cadence ChipStack/AgentStack, Synopsys AgentEngineer, Siemens Fuse EDA AI, all on NVIDIA Nemotron and OpenShell as of DAC 2026 (section 1).
- **Handoff and protocol research.** "Agentic EDA: A Handoff Perspective" (CUHK, 2026-06) defines stage-, flow- and organization-bound handoff validity and proposes a five-layer EDA Agent Communication Protocol (discovery, messaging, tool invocation, orchestration, security/IP) [arXiv 2606.19795](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.19795). Knowledge graphs for agentic formal verification [arXiv 2605.06434](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.06434).
- **Maturity:** research for open flows; early product for vendor flows. **Textbook relevance:** a Verilator + cocotb + coverage loop is reproducible on a laptop and is the right lab vehicle.

## 4. Classical ML in verification (still current)

- **Coverage prediction.** Google's **Design2Vec** (NeurIPS 2021) [peer-reviewed]: GNN over RTL predicts whether a test covers a point with over 90 percent accuracy on an industrial TPU design, usable as a proxy simulator [NeurIPS 2021](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/c5aa65949d20f6b20e1a922c13d974e7-Paper.pdf).
- **Regression test selection and prioritization.** Intel, "RTL regression test selection using machine learning" (ASP-DAC 2022) and "ML to assign debug priorities" (DVCon US 2023) [DVCon 2023 papers](https://archive.dvcon.org/2023-accepted-papers/); DVCon US 2024 session on classifying likely-to-pass tests from history [DVCon 2024 sessions](https://dvcon.org/technical-sessions-2024); DVCon US 2025 "Scalable Gray-Box Instance-Based Reachability Predictor for Automated DV Regression Scheduling" and "Stimulus Diversification and Coverage Closure of 3D NAND Flash with AI" [DVCon 2025 proceedings](https://dvcon-proceedings.org/event_year/y2025/); Qualcomm, "Coverage Prediction and Testcase Generation Framework using ML" (DVCon India 2025) [paper list](https://dvcon-india.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/DVCon_India_2024_Selected_Papers_List.pdf).
- **Coverage closure by optimization.** **NOVA** (2025-11): coverage-aware test selection plus Bayesian optimization of constraint-solver parameters, up to 2.82x faster convergence [arXiv 2512.00688](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00688); RL stimulus generation [arXiv 2405.19815](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.19815); ML with PyUVM [arXiv 2503.11666](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.11666).
- **Adoption reality.** Bennett and Eder's 2025 review of ML in DV since the late 1990s finds coverage closure the most studied task and concludes that most techniques have not reached mainstream adoption, blaming incomparable evaluations and missing open benchmarks [arXiv 2503.11687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11687). The productized exceptions are SimAI and VSO.ai.
- **Textbook relevance:** these are the techniques a DV lead can deploy with regression logs alone; teach them before LLMs.

## 5. Data, IP, trust, and standards

- **Hallucination and vacuity.** Papers repeatedly report hallucinated signal names and unprovable or vacuous SVA; mitigations confine the LLM to existing RTL signals and check assertions by formal proof plus mutation [STELLAR](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.19903), [Quality-aware closure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21451), [AssertLLM2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27472).
- **Security.** Knechtel, Sinanoglu and Karri (2026-05) catalogue data contamination, adversarial evasion and multi-agent vulnerability extraction, and call for dynamic benchmarks and red-teaming [arXiv 2605.10807](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10807v4); HarmChip jailbreak benchmark [arXiv 2604.17093](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.17093v1). Vendors answer with on-prem models and the NVIDIA OpenShell sandbox; whether customers accept cloud models for proprietary RTL is described as unresolved [Unite.AI, 2026-07-26](https://www.unite.ai/synopsys-hands-chip-verification-to-autonomous-ai-agents/).
- **Benchmark limits.** Contamination (VeriContaminated), toy-scale modules under 76 lines (ChipBench critique), and the gap between coverage and bug detection (GRPO test-plan paper, mutation studies) [arXiv 2601.07593](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07593).
- **Sign-off and human-in-the-loop.** Mike Bartley (Alpinum, 2026-06) argues local productivity gains do not equal sign-off evidence; bounded tasks (failure clustering, log summaries, coverage-gap analysis, triage) are ready, judgement tasks are not; defines a seven-element pilot template [Alpinum, 2026-06-19](https://alpinumconsulting.com/blogs/electronics-world-ai-design-verification-measurable-capability/). Cadence's own brief is titled "Agentic AI in Simulation: More Value, Less Hype" [Cadence tech brief](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/resources/technical-briefs/agentic-ai-simulation-more-value-less-hype-tb.html).
